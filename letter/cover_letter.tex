\opening{To \RecipientName,}

Please find enclosed a revision of the manuscript entitled: "Machine Learning
Application to Single Channel Design of Molten Salt Reactor" which I and my
coauthor (Dr. Kathryn D. Huff) are submitting for exclusive consideration for
publication as a research article in Annals of Nuclear Energy.

This manuscript describes and demonstrates a machine learning-enabled nuclear
reactor core design recommendation approach in the context of single channel
design of Molten Salt Reactor (MSR).  We have developed a python tool to
generate a reactor database, to train a predictive model for the estimation of
performance metrics, and to find the optimum designs. The computational workflow
additionally leverages the scikit-learn tool for machine learning algorithms,
the non-dominated sorting genetic algorithm (NSGAIII), and the SERPENT 2 Monte
Carlo software for neutron transport.

Thank you for your consideration of our work. I expect it will be of interest to
a broad readership concerned with the nuclear reactor design, advanced reactors,
optimization, and neutronic simulations to nuclear engineering.  Please address
correspondence concerning this manuscript to me at the University of Illinois.
